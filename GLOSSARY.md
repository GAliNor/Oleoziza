# Oleoziza

Management of one owner's olive oil mills (Sites): receiving and pressing olives, buying olives, selling oil, and following each client's account.

## People

**Management**:
The mill owner and managers, who monitor every activity and administer the mill.
_Avoid_: Admin, boss

**Operator**:
Mill staff who record the day-to-day activity (receptions, weighings, pressings) as it happens.
_Avoid_: Agent, employee, worker

**Client**:
An Individual or a Company that brings olives to a Site, either to have them pressed (Service Milling) or to sell them (Purchase), or both; shared across all Sites.
_Avoid_: Customer, farmer, supplier, account

**Individual**:
A Client who is a private person.
_Avoid_: Particulier, person, natural person

**Company**:
A Client that is a legal entity, with its own legal identity and contact persons.
_Avoid_: Entreprise, business, organisation

**Permission**:
A single right (such as confirming Payments) that Management grants to a user on top of their role.
_Avoid_: Right, privilege, access

## Activities

**Service Milling**:
Pressing a Client's own olives for a fee; the Client takes back the resulting oil.
_Avoid_: Trituration à façon, custom pressing

**Purchase**:
The mill buying olives from a Client.
_Avoid_: Achat, buy, procurement

**Reception**:
The arrival of a Client's olives at the mill, recorded with their variety and whether they are for Service Milling or a Purchase.
_Avoid_: Réception, arrival, intake

**Weighing**:
One measurement of olives at the scale (gross minus tare gives net weight); a Reception can have several.
_Avoid_: Pesée, weight

**Daily Price**:
The price per kg of olives that Management sets for the day's Purchases.
_Avoid_: Rate, tariff

**Product**:
Oil the mill sells, either in bulk or bottled.
_Avoid_: Article, item, SKU

**Lot**:
The olives from one reception, pressed on their own so the resulting oil and yield belong to a single Client.
_Avoid_: Batch, load

**Pressing**:
The crushing of one Lot into oil, recorded with the oil quantity obtained.
_Avoid_: Trituration, milling, extraction

**Yield**:
The oil obtained from a Lot as a percentage of its net olive weight.
_Avoid_: Rendement, ratio

**Oil Stock**:
Oil belonging to a Client that is kept at the mill until they withdraw it.
_Avoid_: Storage, deposit

**Mill Stock**:
Oil owned by the mill itself (from Purchases, its own olives, or oil-share Payments), from which Products are sold.
_Avoid_: Inventory, own stock

**Withdrawal**:
A Client taking some or all of their Oil Stock out of the mill.
_Avoid_: Pickup, delivery, collection

## Money

**Payment**:
Money or oil a Client settles with the mill, by cash, share of oil, bank transfer or cheque; it is only final once confirmed.
_Avoid_: Settlement, transaction

**Payment Proof**:
An image attached to a Payment as evidence (receipt, transfer slip, cheque photo).
_Avoid_: Justificatif, attachment, receipt

**Credit Note**:
An amount the mill owes a Client after a cancellation, to be deducted from future Payments.
_Avoid_: Avoir, credit

**Refund**:
Money the mill gives back to a Client after a cancellation.
_Avoid_: Reimbursement, chargeback

## Accountability

**Audit Trail**:
The record of who did what, when, on which Site, for every change in the app.
_Avoid_: History, log, traçabilité

## Time

**Season**:
One olive harvest campaign (roughly November to February), opened and closed by Management; activity and figures are grouped by it.
_Avoid_: Campagne, campaign, year

## Areas

**Site**:
One mill owned by the owner, with its own Operators, activity, stock and treasury.
_Avoid_: Mill, factory, branch, workspace


**Workspace**:
The area of the app dedicated to one role (Management, Operator).
_Avoid_: Dashboard, site, mill

**Client Space**:
The Client's own portal, where they follow their deliveries, oil and balance.
_Avoid_: Client workspace, client portal

**Backoffice**:
The part of the Management Workspace used to administer the mill (users, prices, products, settings).
_Avoid_: Admin panel, settings
